Bespoke consulting for the music industry.

Hello, I'm Tom — a London born, Yorkshire raised, Los Angeles based music and marketing executive, with 16+ years building and leading digital strategy for independent music across campaigns, platforms, data and teams on both sides of the Atlantic — through the streaming boom, social media's rise and the explosion of available data.

I'm most interested in the nexus of online marketing, owned audience growth and eCommerce — real results over optics, and turning the what, where, why and how into language that actually helps artists, managers and executives make decisions.

That work has helped drive a Billboard Top 3 album, GRAMMY wins and multiple nominations, a Mercury Prize win, and a run of UK Top 5 chart placements — as well as building smart, efficient and capable teams across multiple disciplines.

I have a fiercely independent mindset, which continues to form the core of my approach to work and people.

If you need a hand with anything I can do, are looking to hire someone on a contract/freelance basis, or are just looking to chat about the latest changes in the music industry, let's talk.
